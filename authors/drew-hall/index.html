Drew Hall

Drew Hall is the Founder and Chief Architect of OpticWise and co-author of the Amazon Best Seller Peak Property Performance® (Fast Company Press). He has spent his career in the field-level realities of CRE data and digital infrastructure — the gap between what vendors promise on the slide and what owners actually receive.

He writes and speaks for the operators who want clarity over complexity, and ownership over rent — and co-hosts the Peak Property Performance® Podcast with Bill Douglas.

Credentials

The architect behind owner-controlled CRE infrastructure.

Drew founded OpticWise in 2004 around a single conviction: data & digital infrastructure inside a CRE property is not a vendor utility, it is an owned asset class. He has built the operating standard, the reference architecture, and the field playbook that turn that conviction into a portfolio-scale practice.

  • Founder & Chief Architect, OpticWise — designed the OpticWise reference architecture for owner-controlled CRE data & digital infrastructure; runs the engineering practice across the portfolio.
  • Co-Author, Peak Property Performance® — Amazon Best Seller published by Fast Company Press. Brings the practitioner's voice to the 5C™ plan, the Two-Layer Model, BoT® as Layer 1, and Property Brain™ → Portfolio Brain™ as Layer 2.
  • Co-Host, Peak Property Performance® Podcast — on-the-record conversations with CRE owners, operators, and practitioners.
  • Author, OpticWise Insights — bylined alongside Bill Douglas across the OpticWise publication and the LinkedIn newsletters The Owner's Standard and The Foundation.
