CEO, OpticWise · Co-Author, Peak Property Performance®

Bill Douglas

Bill Douglas is the CEO of OpticWise and co-author of the Amazon Best Seller Peak Property Performance® (Fast Company Press). A lifetime entrepreneur with more than 30 years in technology, he has spent the last 12 applying that experience to commercial real estate — helping global owners convert fragmented, vendor-controlled building technology into owner-controlled data and digital infrastructure that compounds value across a portfolio.

He co-hosts the Peak Property Performance® Podcast with Drew Hall, and coaches executives as the ResilienceGuy.

Credentials

30+ years in technology. 12 in commercial real estate.
