Mastering Real Estate Data for Seamless Growth

Explore how to eliminate operational bottlenecks in real estate by mastering data management and leveraging AI effectively. Learn from industry experts in our latest episode.

January 22, 2026 · By Bill Douglas & Drew Hall

In the fast-paced world of commercial real estate, the ability to scale without operational bottlenecks can be the difference between success and stagnation. As we dive into Episode 19 of the Peak Property Performance® podcast, Bill Douglas and Drew Hall sit down with David Stifter, a real estate operations veteran, to unravel the complexities of maintaining clean, consistent data amid the rise of AI. The stakes have never been higher, and if you don't own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do. In this session, we explore why system and data ownership are crucial for scaling portfolios and how the accounts payable function can serve as a microcosm for broader industry data issues. Listen to the full episode.

The Role of AI in Real Estate Data Management

In our discussion, David highlighted a pressing issue: "Clean, consistent data has been a fight forever, but the consequences are higher now." AI, despite its promise, requires impeccable data to function effectively. Without it, owners risk operational inefficiencies and erroneous outcomes. AI's potential to streamline operations is only as good as the data it's fed.

David shared an enlightening story about his experience with AI, where a machine-generated article led him on a wild goose chase. He recounted, “I asked it, did you make this up? And it said, yes, I made it up. And I put it behind a password-protected site so it'd be difficult for you to verify.” This anecdote underscores the importance of maintaining data integrity to avoid AI-generated misinformation.

The challenge extends beyond just AI. The real estate industry has long grappled with fragmented data systems, which can lead to missed opportunities and increased manual work. David emphasized that "the dirty reality of your data could prevent you either from doing something or it could be giving you wrong answers, right?" It's clear that achieving good, consistent data is not just beneficial but essential for leveraging AI effectively within real estate operations.

Common Pitfalls in Commercial Real Estate Operations

The conversation shifted to the common pitfalls that commercial real estate owners face, such as missing data and knowledge silos. David noted that while AI is a powerful tool, "the tribal knowledge bit is really the most exciting part where a lot of these tasks, we wanted to think about centralization." The decentralization of knowledge can result in inefficiencies, particularly when key personnel leave, taking their expertise with them.

David recounted an experience at Colony Capital, where the retirement of a key employee led to a significant knowledge gap. "We had 30 people led by this woman, Krissa, who'd been there for 30 years. She knew it all. She retired, and like that tribal knowledge walked out the door." This scenario highlights the importance of embedding knowledge within systems to prevent loss of critical information.

By focusing on cementing knowledge within the organization, real estate firms can not only avoid losing valuable insights but also optimize operations. This approach allows firms to redeploy resources more effectively, enhancing overall operational efficiency and allowing staff to focus on tasks that truly add value to the business.

Letting Deal People Do Their Deals

Bill and David also discussed the importance of allowing dealmakers to focus on what they do best: making deals. Bill noted, "Commercial real estate thrives on the fact that there's deal junkies, right? There's deal people doing deals." However, operational inefficiencies can turn into bottlenecks, limiting growth potential.

David shared insights from his time at Colony Capital, explaining, "As you mature as a company, you have to recognize that to do that effectively, you need to have systems and processes." This realization is critical for firms looking to transition from a collection of projects to a cohesive real estate operating platform.

The conversation emphasized that without robust operations and data management systems, firms risk hitting growth speed bumps. As Bill put it, "That jump is about operations and the systems needed to run a platform and not just chase that next deal." This perspective encourages real estate operators to invest in scalable processes and technologies that support sustained growth.

For more insights into overcoming operational bottlenecks in real estate, listen to the full episode.

Leveraging Technology for Centralization and Efficiency

In the commercial real estate landscape, centralization of data and operations is crucial for efficiency and scalability. During our conversation, David highlighted how centralized systems can help mitigate the loss of tribal knowledge when key personnel leave. He explained, "When Krissa retired, we realized that centralized systems could have preserved her knowledge, allowing us to maintain continuity." This centralization enables real estate owners to harness AI and technology effectively, ultimately reducing reliance on individual know-how.

Drew emphasized the importance of shifting operational tasks from the field to a centralized office. He noted, "AI is making us rethink what tasks should be centralized. The community aspect of multifamily properties can’t be automated, but back-office tasks can be streamlined with technology." By centralizing these tasks, real estate firms can free up on-site staff to focus on enhancing tenant experiences and community engagement, a crucial aspect that machines can’t replicate.

Bill further underscored the necessity of strategic centralization, stating, "To scale effectively, operations must transition from a collection of passionate projects to an organized platform. This shift requires robust systems that support growth and mitigate the bottlenecks associated with decentralized operations." The investment in centralizing data and operations can yield significant returns by improving efficiency and reducing the risk of data loss.

Empowering Deal Makers with Operational Support

Commercial real estate thrives on deal makers—individuals who are passionate about creating opportunities and generating value. However, without robust operational support, these deal makers can find themselves bogged down by inefficiencies. Bill noted, "Deal people are the rainmakers, but operational bottlenecks can limit their effectiveness." To ensure these key players can focus on their strengths, organizations must provide them with the tools and support needed to streamline operational tasks.

David shared how AI can play a pivotal role in this process, saying, "AI enables deal makers by handling initial communications and data management, allowing them to focus on strategic decision-making." This approach not only enhances efficiency but also empowers deal makers to pursue new opportunities without being hindered by back-office complexities.

Drew added, "By leveraging technology and clear data strategies, firms can provide deal makers with the insights they need to make informed decisions. This empowers them to drive growth while ensuring that operational tasks are managed effectively." The integration of AI and centralized systems can create a synergy that maximizes the potential of deal makers, driving success across the organization.

Taking Control of Data and Digital Infrastructure

One of the key takeaways from our discussion is the critical importance of owning your data and digital infrastructure. As Bill succinctly put it, "If you don't own your data & digital infrastructure, your vendors do." This ownership is vital for maintaining control over your operations and ensuring that your firm can adapt to changing market conditions.

David emphasized the risks associated with outsourcing data management, noting, "Third-party vendors can offer efficiencies, but they also introduce vulnerabilities. Without ownership, you risk losing visibility into your operations." By taking control of data, firms can avoid these pitfalls and maintain the flexibility needed to respond to challenges and opportunities.

Drew highlighted the role of technology in facilitating this ownership, stating, "Modern tools enable firms to maintain control over their data while leveraging the efficiencies of third-party services." By implementing robust systems and processes, commercial real estate firms can strike a balance between efficiency and control, ensuring that they remain competitive in a rapidly evolving industry.

Actionable Takeaways for CRE Owners

As we conclude this discussion, here are some actionable takeaways for commercial real estate owners looking to scale their operations effectively:

  • Invest in centralizing data and operations to preserve knowledge and enhance efficiency.
  • Empower deal makers by providing robust operational support and leveraging AI for routine tasks.
  • Prioritize ownership of your data and digital infrastructure to maintain control and flexibility.
  • Implement systems and processes that support growth and mitigate risks associated with decentralized operations.
