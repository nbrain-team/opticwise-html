It Wasn’t Magic—It Was Smart Infrastructure

When budgets are tight and staff are stretched thin, most operators don’t need a revolution—they need a single smart move that removes friction across the board.

In this case? That move saved over $120,000/year.


Meet the Portfolio:

  • 6 buildings
  • 3 ops staff
  • A mess of legacy access, lighting, and HVAC systems

Before OpticWise:

  • 4 different vendors
  • 20+ monthly service calls
  • 2-hour daily reactive checks

After:

  • Single-pane control
  • Automated lighting + HVAC tied to real-time occupancy
  • Mobile access + system alerts


Savings: $10K/month in labor + energy + vendor consolidationn

Service calls down: 40%
