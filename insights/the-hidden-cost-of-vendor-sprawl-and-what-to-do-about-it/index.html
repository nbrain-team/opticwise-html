You’re Managing Your Vendors, Not Your Building

As an operator, your day shouldn’t be a vendor triage. Yet too often, it is. Multiple platforms, invoices, service calls, and contracts—none integrated, all demanding attention.

That chaos has a name: vendor sprawl. And it’s not just inconvenient—it’s eroding your operational efficiency, increasing risk, and dragging down NOI.


“True operational leverage comes not from adding more systems—but from consolidating them.” -Peak Property Performance

Why Vendor Sprawl Undermines Your Ops

  • Juggling multiple vendors inflates labor and admin overhead.
  • Disconnected systems lead to blind spots and reactive workflows.
  • Variability in service quality creates tenant friction and compliance risks.

According to ApartmentBuildings.com, “Juggling multiple vendors creates inefficiencies, service inconsistencies, and administrative headaches.” Katharos Consultancy+1ApartmentBuildings.Com

Story: From Chaos to Coherent Control

One mid-sized office portfolio struggled with:

  • 8 different vendors handling access, lighting, cleaning, and metering
  • 30+ service requests monthly
  • Limited oversight across systems

After implementing a vendor consolidation strategy based on Peak Property Performance frameworks:

  • Vendor count dropped by 60%
  • Administrative time decreased 30%
  • Ticket volume halved—improving tenant satisfaction

Vendor Sprawl Pitfalls—and How to Fix Them

3 Steps to Own Your Ops Back

  1. Audit your tech stack: List all vendors, systems, and platforms.
  2. Identify overlap and risk: Where’s friction, cost, or system failure happening?
  3. Consolidate and monitor: Shift to integrated vendors, unify access and automation, and track performance metrics centrally.
