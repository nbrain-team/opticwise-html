What It Is

Who owns what
Across every building system and every vendor contract.

Inside the Clarify Step
How the PPP Audit™ Runs
The Audit runs in five short stages inside the Clarify step.
Step 01
Property Intake
30 min Baseline information: systems, vendors, contracts, current challenges.
Step 02
Data & Digital Mapping
Working session Where data lives, who controls it, operational burden vs. KPIs.
Step 03
Data & Digital Infrastructure Assessment
Working session Connectivity, segmentation, access control, governance posture.
Step 04
Gap Analysis
Deliverable Where you have control, where you delegated it, what each gap costs.
Step 05
Roadmap Presentation
Deliverable Prioritized roadmap: quick wins, medium-term, long-term.
“ Clarify first. Scale with control. ”

Property Data Map

Control Gap Analysis

Prioritized Roadmap
